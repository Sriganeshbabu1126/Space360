# Space360 User Manual

Welcome to Space360! This guide will help you navigate and utilize the platform effectively.

## 1. Login Instructions

To access Space360:
1. Navigate to the login page.
2. Enter your registered email address and password.
3. Click **Login**.
4. If you have forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" link to reset it.

## 2. Project Setup

After logging in, you can access your assigned projects.
1. Go to the **Dashboard**.
2. Select the desired project from the project list.
3. You will be directed to the project's main workspace, where you can view all associated assets and information.

## 3. Video Upload Workflow

To upload a new 360° video to a project:
1. Open the project workspace.
2. Click on the **Upload Video** button.
3. Select the 360° video file from your local machine.
4. Add relevant metadata (e.g., date, location, description).
5. Click **Upload** and wait for the processing to complete. You will receive a notification once the video is ready for viewing.

## 4. 360° Viewer Navigation

The 360° viewer allows you to explore the uploaded videos immersively:
- **Click and Drag:** Click anywhere on the video and drag your mouse to look around in 360 degrees.
- **Zoom In/Out:** Use the scroll wheel on your mouse to zoom in and out.
- **Playback Controls:** Use the play, pause, and progress bar controls at the bottom of the viewer to navigate through the video timeline.

## 5. Path Overlay Explanation

The path overlay feature displays the route taken during the video recording on the project's floor plan.
- The overlay is represented by a colored line on the floor plan map.
- As the video plays, a marker moves along the path to indicate the current location within the facility.
- You can click anywhere on the path to jump the video to that specific location.

## 6. Issue Markup Tutorial

You can highlight and annotate specific issues directly within the 360° viewer:
1. Pause the video at the location of the issue.
2. Click the **Add Issue** button in the viewer toolbar.
3. Click on the specific area in the video where the issue is located.
4. A markup dialogue will appear. Enter a title, description, and assign a priority level.
5. Click **Save**. The issue will now be marked with a pin in the viewer and listed in the project's issue tracker.
